### Modern Web Guidance

- **`dialog`**: I implemented modal dialogs using native `<dialog>` and `showModal()` without redundant manual focus traps or ARIA modal attributes, containing scroll gestures on `dialog:modal` and `dialog::backdrop` ("stops scroll gestures in the dialog chaining to the page").
- **`light-dismiss-a-dialog`**: I declared `closedby="any"` on the modal dialogs and added a bounding box coordinate check fallback ("Check if the click coordinates fall within the dialog's content box") to ensure clicking the backdrop cleanly dismisses modals across all browsers without closing when interior content is clicked.
- **`dark-mode`**: I placed `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` in the document head alongside an inline FOUC prevention script ("place a <meta> tag in your <head> to ensure the browser knows which themes you support before it even starts rendering") to guarantee the browser renders the correct canvas and form control themes prior to client execution.
- **`ime-safe-enter-submit`**: In the clan search input and compatibility forms, I intercepted `Enter` keydown events by verifying `event.isComposing || event.keyCode === 229` ("During IME composition, browsers report keyCode: 229 (VK_PROCESSKEY) instead of 13 on keydown") to prevent premature submissions during character conversion.
